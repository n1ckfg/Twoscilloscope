three.twoscilloscope

Vector shapes to oscilloscope audio and back again in three.js: a port of ofxTwoscilloscope. The browser starts the sound on the first click or key press.

  • example-encode: vector shapes -> XYscope format audio.
  • example-decode: XYscope format audio -> an analog scope beam and vector shapes.
  • example-transform: vector shape -> XY audio -> audio effects -> new vector shape.
  • example-latk: a 3D Latk animation through the same round trip, drawn as beams or strokes.
